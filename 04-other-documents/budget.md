# Budget

`[VERIFY]` Ceiling for the 2026 call: **~CHF 250,000 in project funds over 4 years**, plus the
grantee's salary. This is **down from CHF 400,000** in earlier calls. Older successful
applications you may be shown as models were written against the higher ceiling — do not copy
their staffing.

`[VERIFY]` Salary placeholder for budgeting: ~**CHF 115,000/year** (earlier guidance: ~105,000).

## What this ceiling means in practice

CHF 250,000 over four years does not fund a large team. Realistically it is roughly one doctoral
student for a substantial part of the grant, plus consumables and travel — and the exact
arithmetic depends on your institution's salary scales, which your grants office can give you.

The design consequence is important and should be faced now rather than in October: **a project
scoped for the old ceiling will not fit.** If your plan needs two PhD students plus a postdoc,
you must either descope, or bring institutional co-funding, or restructure the work packages so
that the funded person is doing the critical path and other contributions are secured elsewhere.
Reviewers assess whether the requested resources match the proposed work in both directions —
an over-ambitious plan on a small budget reads as poor feasibility judgement just as clearly as
a padded budget reads as poor stewardship.
