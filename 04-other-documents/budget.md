# Budget for mySNF (Ambizione 2026), working version of 20 October 2026, third pass

Two things are entered in mySNF. The applicant's salary, which the grant pays separately, and the
project funds, capped at CHF 250,000 over four years including social charges. The budget is
line by line and frozen at submission. Each category has a "remarques" box, and the RGO advises
to justify every amount there and to point to the plan sections (2.3.3 workplan, 2.4 resources,
2.5 schedule). Open-access publication costs are not eligible here (separate mechanism).

Support staff are ARE only (auxiliaires de recherche et d'enseignement), as the RGO indicated on
14 Oct and as the plan's table in 2.3.3 already says ("Research assistants, master's students,
at most 40%"). An ARE is a student enrolled in a bachelor or a master, without a master's degree
at hiring, paid at class 6 annuity 0 of the Geneva scale. The class-17 collaborator considered
earlier on 20 Oct is dropped (applicant, 20 Oct, "I was wrong").

Figures marked (assumed) are planning assumptions to confirm with the RGO. Social charges are
23% in Geneva and mySNF adds them automatically. They are shown here so that the total can be
checked against the cap.

### Reasoning on the annuity at the start, September 2027

1. If the 2027 budget passes with the annuity, the applicant moves from 19/9 to 19/10 on
   1 January 2027. If the State runs on twelfths again, he stays at 19/9.
2. The change of function to collaborateur scientifique II is within class 19. The acquired
   salary is protected on a change of function inside the State and the University, so the
   entry annuity cannot be below the current one.
3. At appointment, UNIGE may recognise post-doctoral experience. The RGO's own estimate (19/12
   in year 1) suggests she applied that. HR decides, it cannot be settled here.
4. The amounts below annuity 12 are extrapolated with the top step of 1,049. The scale's early
   steps are larger, so these figures slightly overstate 19/9 to 19/11. mySNF corrects at award.

## 3. Remarks to paste in mySNF (one per category, English)

Staff, research assistant 1 (ARE, 40%, M1 to M48). A master's student, renewed by academic
year, who supports the specified technical work. Loading of the context feeds, alignment of the
outcome series, the rolling replay pipeline of WP3, the observation-mode dashboard runs and the
reproducibility of the evaluation code. The design, the models and the analyses are the
applicant's (plan 2.3.3, 2.4). Doctoral students and postdocs are not employed, in line with the
Ambizione rules.

Staff, research assistant 2 (ARE, 40%, M3 to M38). A master's student who acts as the second
independent extractor of the manual benchmark in T1.2 (M3 to M9) under the registered
protocol, then supports the manual checks of the automated extraction, the episode inventory
and the evaluation of the respiratory and heat tests (plan 2.3.3, T1.2, T1.3, T3.0 to T3.4).

Travel, conferences. Two European conferences per year and one overseas over the grant, to
present the registered results to the forecasting and evidence-synthesis communities (ESCAIDE,
Epidemics, International Symposium on Forecasting, ISBA, ICASR). CHF 2,000 per European
conference and CHF 4,000 overseas (plan 2.6, statement of mobility).

Travel, mobility. Two short stays of one to two weeks with groups working on forecast evaluation
and nowcasting, hosts to be arranged once first results exist. Two visiting researchers hosted
in Geneva to reuse the open benchmark and code (statement of mobility, Regulations Art. 9 §4c).

Workshops. One elicitation workshop with two to five experienced people from the Geneva
emergency services to set the costs of wrong decisions (plan 2.3.3, T4.1), and one final
briefing of results to the data partners (plan 2.6).

IT services. Set-up of the project database and of the secure-environment runs with UNIGE IT
services, and a small instance for the daily dashboard in observation mode for the demand tests
that pass validation and that the services authorise (plan 2.3.3, T3.6 and WP4, 2.4).

Open research data. Deposit and curation of the open extraction benchmark (D1.1), the code and
the evaluation outputs in a public repository with a persistent identifier (plan 2.6). Budgeted
now since it cannot be added later.

Data access and regulatory fees. Extraction and preparation of the emergency series by the HUG
data office, and ethics commission fees for the Art. 34 LRH authorisation or clarification
(plan 2.4, data governance).

## 5. Rules from the call documents (unchanged)

- Project funds capped at CHF 250,000 for four years (Art. 10 §4), pro-rated if shorter.
- Applicant salary separate, work quota 100% (80% only for care duties or further
  qualification). The SNSF confirms the exact salary with the institution for approved grants.
- Doctoral students and postdocs cannot be employed. Other staff must be justified in the plan
  (Guidelines 4.3), which 2.4 does.
- Equipment of enduring value at most CHF 100,000, not a live constraint.
- Open-access publication costs not eligible here. Open research data costs eligible, to be
  budgeted now.
- No budget change after submission.
