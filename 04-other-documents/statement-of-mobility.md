# Statement of mobility

`[HIGH]` Required, and **evaluated**. Two jobs: justify the choice of research institution, and
present your past and planned mobility.

`[VERIFY]` Length limit — check the Call 2026 document. Plan for roughly one page unless told
otherwise.

## Structure

**1. Past mobility.** Institutions, countries, dates, and — the part applicants skip — *what
each move bought you scientifically*. A list of places is not an argument. "Two years at X,
where I learned method Y, which is central to WP2" is.

**2. Why this institution for this project.** The heart of it. Specific and verifiable:
named collaborators, infrastructure, cohorts, archives, computing, a local community that makes
this project stronger here than elsewhere. Generic praise of the institution's excellence is
worth nothing and reads as filler.

**3. Planned mobility during the grant.** Research stays, collaborations, exchanges,
international network building.

## If you are staying at your current institution

Permitted, and common, but you carry a burden of proof. Make the argument positive rather than
defensive: this is the only place with the facility/cohort/collaborator that the project needs;
your prior mobility already delivered breadth; you are moving to a *different* group,
department or role within the institution; you have concrete outgoing mobility planned during
the grant. Do not write a paragraph that boils down to inertia — reviewers read that as a
signal about ambition, fairly or not.

## If you are moving

Easier to argue, but the letters must match. The host institution's detailed confirmation letter
and this statement should be visibly consistent about what you are being given and why you are
being taken.
