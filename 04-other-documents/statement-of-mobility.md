# Statement of mobility

`[HIGH]` Required, and **evaluated**. Two jobs: justify the choice of research institution, and
present your past and planned mobility.

`[VERIFY]` Length limit — check the Call 2026 guidelines. Plan for about one page.

---

## Your situation — read this first

MSc (2004), DEA (2006), PhD (2023) and current position all at the University of Geneva, and the
plan is to apply from UNIGE. **This is the weakest element of the dossier**, and a referee will
see it immediately from the CV without needing to look for it.

It is not disqualifying — Ambizione does not require a move — but it means this one-page document
carries more weight for you than for most applicants, and it cannot be written as a formality.
Two things make it defensible:

**1. A positive, specific, verifiable argument for Geneva.** Not "UNIGE is excellent". Rather:
this project requires case-level surveillance data linked to individual SIG hot water
installations; that linkage exists nowhere else in Switzerland; it exists because of agreements
with SIG, OCEN and the Service du Médecin Cantonal that took years to build; and the ethics
approval covering it (BASEC 2026-00324) is already granted. That is a genuine, checkable argument
that this project is only possible here. Very few applicants can make a claim that concrete —
lead with it.

**2. Real, planned, agreed outgoing mobility during the grant.** This is what you currently lack
and what has to be built in August, because it depends on other people saying yes. Named host,
agreed period, and a role that is visible in a work package — a three-month stay that WP3 depends
on is worth more than any amount of narrative.

Candidate partners, chosen so the stay does scientific work rather than decorating the statement:

- **Swiss National Reference Centre for Legionella** (Bellinzona) — clinical and environmental
  isolate expertise, national perspective.
- **SwissLEGIO** network (ISPM Bern / Unisanté) — national surveillance and source attribution;
  also the natural route to multi-canton data for WP1.
- **Empa** or **EPFL building physics** — the mechanistic exposure layer in WP3. This one also
  broadens you beyond epidemiology, which helps the independence argument.
- **RIVM** (Netherlands) — among the strongest groups internationally on Legionella and water
  systems.
- **ECDC / ELDSNet** — European surveillance perspective.

A further honest point: your prior career gives you a form of *sectoral* mobility — fifteen years
in quantitative finance before moving into biomedical research, a change of discipline more
radical than most geographic moves. That is a legitimate argument and it should be made
explicitly, because a reader who does not have it pointed out will simply see a CV that never
left Geneva. Make it as a genuine argument about breadth of method and perspective, not as an
excuse offered in place of mobility.

---

## Structure

**1. Past mobility and trajectory.** Institutions, dates, and what each move bought you
scientifically. Include the sector change (finance → global health) as a substantive move, with
what it brought: econometric identification, risk and tail modelling, cost-effectiveness — the
methods that carry WP2 and WP4.

**2. Why Geneva for this project.** The data linkage, the SIG/OCEN/cantonal partnerships, the
existing ethics approval, the Campus Biotech environment. Specific and verifiable throughout.

**3. Planned mobility during the grant.** Named hosts, agreed periods, and the work package each
stay serves. Get at least one confirmed in writing before submission.
