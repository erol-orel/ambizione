## Olivia Keiser (contact person ask): 10 minutes

**The ask, first sentence.** "Je dépose une candidature Ambizione le 3 novembre; accepterais-tu
d'être ma personne de contact sur la confirmation d'hébergement? Signature d'ici mardi 14."

**The blocking question, second sentence.** "Le règlement limite chaque personne de contact à
UNE candidature par appel; quelqu'un d'autre te l'a-t-il déjà demandé?" If yes: stop, thank
her, tell me the same day; we go to the RGO for an alternative.

**What she signs.** The SNSF template verbatim: hosting commitments + the independence
guarantees (your scientific direction, your team choices, your budget, your senior
authorship) + the one-applicant acknowledgment + one sentence that the programme runs
independently alongside the groups. Nothing to draft; the letter is ready.

**The objection she might raise: "why not simply inside IDMM?"** Answer, honestly: the SNSF
evaluates the transition to independence; the scheme's own template makes the host guarantee
the applicant's autonomy. The project stays physically and scientifically close to the group;
what changes is accountability: my programme, my budget, my last-author line. Her signature as
contact person is exactly the supervisor's role UNIGE's own form expects.

**What she gets.** Zero drafting, zero praise letter (the SNSF discards those); transparency:
the plan names her roles in full and states she has no scientific role in this project.

constraint, not my draft.
