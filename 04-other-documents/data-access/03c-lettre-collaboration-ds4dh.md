**Subject: letter of support - COLDSTART project, SNSF Ambizione application (Dr Erol Orel)**

Dear Madam, dear Sir,

We hereby confirm the collaboration of the **Data Science for Digital Health (DS4DH) group,
Department of Radiology and Medical Informatics, University of Geneva**, with Dr Erol Orel
in the context of the **COLDSTART** project, submitted to the 2026 Ambizione call of the
Swiss National Science Foundation.

Our group has been collaborating with Dr Orel since 2025 within the Interreg GESICA project.

Within the COLDSTART project, we confirm our intention to continue this collaboration as the
**methodological collaboration partner for its evidence-extraction work package (WP1)**:
regular scientific exchange on information retrieval, in particular on the design of the extraction benchmark and the characterisation
of automated extraction error. This collaboration involves scientific exchange only and
carries no financial implication for our group.

We remain at your disposal to specify the practical modalities of this collaboration.
