## Statement of mobility

**Erol Orel** · Ambizione 2026 · Host: Institute of Global Health, Faculty of Medicine,
University of Geneva

### Opening remark (use in the form's general/first field, or dimension 1)

My degrees and my current position are at the University of Geneva, and the proposed host is the
institute where I work. I state this at the outset rather than leave it to be inferred, and I
address the choice directly below. My mobility case rests on a sectoral discontinuity most
careers do not contain, on international scientific networks built without relocation, and on a
concrete organisational change of position - not on a change of address, and not on invented
travel.

### 1. Institutional academic mobility

**Retrospective.** My institutional trajectory runs across communities rather than cities. After
a master's in quantitative economics and a DEA in econometrics (2006, supervised by Prof. Eva
Cantoni), I spent fifteen years in the financial sector before joining the Institute of Global
Health in 2019 for a doctorate in biomedical sciences (defended December 2023). Since then I have
worked across institutional boundaries inside and beyond UNIGE: with the Data Science for Digital
Health group (Department of Radiology and Medical Informatics) on biomedical NLP; with HUG
emergency medicine and the CASU-144 on operational data and a joint systematic review; and with
the cantonal public-health services on the legionellosis study, for which I hold the data lead
under an ethics approval already granted (BASEC 2026-00324).

**The choice of host, stated plainly.** I have been at my present institution for more than a
year, I did my doctorate there, and it is the institution of my doctoral advisor - every reason
the guidelines give for motivating the choice carefully applies to me. The motivation is
concrete: the project's primary outcome series (CASU-144), the emergency-medicine partnerships,
the cantonal linkage under BASEC 2026-00324 and the evidence-extraction platform all live in
Geneva, and no other institution reproduces that combination. What changes is my **position**:
Ambizione would establish an independent research programme that I lead, alongside the
Institute's existing groups rather than inside one, with my own ethics approvals, my own budget
authority and senior authorship - guaranteed in the host confirmation, which is signed at
institute level. The SNSF's own definition of institutional mobility includes collaborations
within the same institution that provide complementary expertise; that is precisely the
DS4DH–HUG–cantonal configuration this project assembles.

**Prospective.** The programme is designed to run across institutions from month 1: DS4DH for
extraction methodology, HUG and CASU-144 for operational data and threshold elicitation, and the
cantonal services for the waterborne extension.

### 2. Transnational academic mobility

**Retrospective.** My networks are international although my address has not been. Doctoral work
on HIV prediction across East and Southern Africa; seroprevalence estimation in Cameroon (*Nature
Communications* 2021); contributions to WHO African Region epidemiological reporting during the
pandemic; since 2024 the Franco-Swiss Interreg consortium **GESICA** (HUG as Swiss lead, CHUV,
University of Neuchâtel, HEIG-VD, Techwan, with French partners `[[Université Marie et Louis
Pasteur, HES-SO - confirm the list you cite]]`); and from 2026 the Horizon Europe project
**GeoAI4EI**, a European epidemic-intelligence toolbox. The SNSF counts involvement in
border-crossing networks without active travel; both consortia are exactly that, with regular
cross-border working meetings.

**Prospective.** Under the grant: continued Franco-Swiss collaboration through GESICA's
operational partners; participation in the GeoAI4EI consortium across `[[member countries]]`;
presentation of the confirmatory results at international conferences `[[name 2–3 target venues
- e.g. epidemic-forecasting and biostatistics conferences you actually attend]]`; and **2–3
short methodological visits** (days to weeks) to groups working on probabilistic forecast
evaluation, nowcasting and Bayesian evidence borrowing `[[name the groups and indicative years -
your decision; the regulations require concrete prospective content, not a list of
possibilities]]`. I will also **host visiting researchers in Geneva**, so the exchange runs in
both directions: the project's benchmark, evidence-to-prior library and evaluation code are open
resources designed for exactly that use.

### 3. Intersectoral mobility

**Retrospective - the strongest dimension.** Fifteen years in the financial sector as a
quantitative analyst and risk manager (2007–2018, including co-founding an asset-management
firm), estimating rare and costly events: extreme-value methods for non-Gaussian tails, regime
and factor models, stress testing, scenario analysis. In 2019 I left that career for biomedical
research. This is a complete change of sector and of field, and it is the source of the
project's methodological core: the regime-switching representation, the tail modelling and the
decision-analytic evaluation were carried across that boundary, not acquired in any research
group I have belonged to. I have since obtained innovation-sector funding for my own instrument
(Venture Kick CHF 10,000; Mimosa CHF 20,000; UNIGE CHF 30,000) - a sectoral bridge run in the
other direction.

**Prospective.** WP4 is built with practice: escalation thresholds elicited from emergency
physicians, dispatch supervisors and capacity managers, and results returned to the operational
partners who supplied the data.

### 4. Interdisciplinary mobility

**Retrospective.** Econometrics → quantitative finance → biomedical sciences → automated
evidence synthesis (LiteRev, *JMIR* 2023; LiteRev-Evidence) → emergency public health. Each move
is documented in published work, and the senior-authored machine-learning classification across
WHO STEPS surveys (accepted) marks the transition from conducting analyses within these fields
to directing them.

**Prospective.** The project itself is the interdisciplinary programme: natural language
processing (WP1), Bayesian time-series and extreme-value modelling (WP2–WP3) and
decision science with clinical partners (WP4), integrated around one falsifiable question.

### 5. Intellectual mobility (communication beyond academia)

**Retrospective.** I teach statistics and epidemiology to practising clinicians and public-health
professionals on the MAS in Public Health (since 2024) - an audience that acts on methods rather
than publishes about them, which shaped this project's decision-first evaluation. During the
pandemic I built the COVID-19 dashboard used for WHO African Region situational awareness. My
platform work is public-facing by design, with operational scenarios elaborated jointly with
emergency-medicine partners.

**Prospective.** The project's outputs are deliberately usable outside academia: an open
extraction benchmark, open software, elicited and documented escalation thresholds returned to
the emergency services, and result briefings to the cantonal partners `[[and, if you wish, a
public-facing summary via the platform - your call]]`. A negative result will be communicated
with the same care as a positive one, because its practical consequence - do not let
literature-derived parameters drive operational decisions - is aimed at practitioners.

   The named short visits in dimension 2 are the one block that still needs your decision;
   the regulations require concrete content there, and "to be identified" will read as a blank.
2. If the form's fields are tight, cut in this order: the Venture Kick/Mimosa sentence in
   dimension 3; the WHO dashboard sentence in dimension 5; the conference-venues clause. Do not
   cut the opening remark, the host-choice paragraph (dimension 1) or the sectoral paragraph
   (dimension 3) - those carry the case.
3. Keep wording consistent with the research plan: "independent research programme alongside the
   Institute's groups", "operational demand signal", the two registration points. Reviewers read
   the statement against the plan (Guidelines 3.3).
4. If asked why no long stay: the scheme permits one but does not require it; the project's
   assets - the 144 series, the partnerships, the cantonal linkage, the platform - are
   concentrated in Geneva and are not reproducible elsewhere, and equivalent mobility is
   achieved through the concrete activities above (Art. 9 §4c).
