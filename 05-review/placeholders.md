## The three that gate everything else

- **`[[N]]`** the cold-start window and **`[[Δ]]`** the non-inferiority margin — both from the
  power simulation, after the data-access checkpoint and episode inventory. Do not guess them.
- **The archetype primary horizons** — 14 days is the working value for respiratory; heat needs its
  own shorter horizon. Same simulation, same timing.

## Everything else is legwork

Names, dates, FTE, salary rates, participant counts, thresholds, ethics status. None of it needs
another scientific decision.
