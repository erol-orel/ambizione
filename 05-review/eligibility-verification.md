## Mobility: what the evidence actually supports

Nothing found says a research stay is **required**. The scheme's own framing is about
leading an independent project, and the SNSF guidelines describe research stays as
permitted and budgetable, not mandatory.

That matters, because the previous plan had drifted into designing a 3–6 month LSHTM
stay to satisfy a criterion rather than because it served the science. That is backwards
and has been removed. The current position:

- **Do not manufacture a move.** A stay proposed to fill a checkbox reads as one.
- The dossier's mobility case rests on **intersectoral and intellectual mobility** —
  fifteen years in quantitative finance, then a change of field — plus international
  collaboration, which is real and documented.
- Geographic mobility is thin and is stated plainly rather than disguised. That is a
  weakness the panel may weigh; it is not a disqualification unless clause 1 above says
  otherwise.
- A short, genuinely useful external exchange can still be included **if you want it**.
  It should not be the centrepiece and should not be invented.

Confirm point 3 in the guidelines. If a stay turns out to be formally required, that
changes the calculus and we revisit; if it is merely evaluated, the honest position
above is the right one.
