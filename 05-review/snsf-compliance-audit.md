# Compliance audit against the actual call documents

Source: `00-source-documents/call-documents/` — Guidelines (v. 11.08.2026), Regulations
(v. 25.02.2026), Written confirmation template (v. 03.08.2026). Now in the repo; previously the
whole dossier rested on secondary sources.

## A. Where I was wrong

**A1. Mobility — my last change was non-compliant.** I removed the research stay entirely and wrote
"none should be invented". **Regulations Art. 9 §3** is explicit:

> If the applicant did not conduct a research stay of 12 months after the doctorate at an
> institution other than the one where he/she obtained the doctorate […] the applicant **must
> explain in the application how he/she plans to achieve mobility that is equivalent in
> qualitative terms under the Ambizione grant.**

That is mandatory content, and it applies. The RGO was right that there is no eligibility bar; it
does not follow that nothing need be proposed. **Art. 9 §4(c)** then permits equivalence through
"other types of mobility, particularly **short visits or international collaborations**" — so the
decision not to relocate stands, but a concrete prospective plan must replace the blank.

**A2. "100% of research time" is not a distinguishing commitment.** Art. 1 §4 and Art. 6 §1(c)
**require** a 100% work quota (80% only for care duties or further qualification). Presenting it as
an independence asset would read as not having read the rules. It is still true that it replaces a
split across four programmes — that framing is fine; "I will devote 100%" as a virtue is not.

**A3. The host signatory is not one person.** Guidelines 2.16 and the template: the detailed
confirmation is signed by **the contact person (head of the research group) named in the
application AND the head of the institute/department** — two signatures. "Ray signs it" is
therefore incomplete, and the contact-person role is a real structural question given that the
applicant is to lead his own programme. See D1.

## D. Decisions that need the Institute

**D1. Who is the "contact person (head of the research group)"?** The detailed confirmation needs
that signature *plus* the head of institute/department. If the applicant leads an independent
programme, he cannot be his own contact person, and whoever signs is spending their **one**
supportable application for the call (Art. 8 §6) — the letter must say they acknowledge this.
Resolve with the Institute before drafting: who signs as contact person, who as head of institute,
and is either already committed to another applicant.

**D2. The letter must state "the project's autonomy compared to other ongoing research objectives
at the research institution."** This is the GESICA / GeoAI4EI / legionellosis delimitation, and the
SNSF asks the *institution* to make it — not the applicant. That is a stronger place for it than
§2.6, and the delimitation table should be handed to whoever drafts the letter.

**D3. Parallel submissions** (Art. 13): no SNSF project, Sinergia, SPIRIT or programme grant may be
requested, approved or ongoing for the same period, as applicant or co-applicant. GESICA (Interreg)
and GeoAI4EI (Horizon Europe) are not SNSF instruments, so both look clear — confirm there is no
SNSF grant on which the applicant is named.

## E. What the guidelines say about the independence weakness

Guidelines §1, on what the scheme expects:

> You should have at least **one major research output independent from your work as a PhD student
> and the associated PhD supervision framework.**

This is the hostile review's F5.1 as an explicit expectation rather than a guess. Two things help:

- **DORA (Guidelines 3.1):** "the scientific quality, value and impact of different research
  outputs (including **datasets, software, prototypes**, research publications)". **LiteRev-Evidence
  is admissible as a major achievement in its own right** — built after the doctorate, on
  independently raised funding. It should be presented as a research output, not only as
  infrastructure.
- Guidelines 3.2 requires "a brief description of your own contribution in referenced
  publications". Use it to state authorship roles precisely on the co-authored record.

## F. Choice of institution — four triggers, all of them ours

Guidelines 2.15 and mobility dimension 1 require the choice to be **carefully motivated** if:

1. more than one year already spent at the present Swiss institution — **yes**;
2. more than one year as postdoc at the selected institution — **yes**;
3. returning to the institution of the doctorate — **yes**;
4. joining the institution of the PhD advisor — **yes**.

All four. This is not a detail to handle in passing; it is the single most scrutinised part of this
particular application, and it belongs in the mobility statement, argued rather than mentioned.

**One finding that helps materially.** Dimension 1, institutional academic mobility, is defined as
collaborations "on a national level **or even within the same research institution** providing, for
instance, complementary expertise, or additional infrastructure." The organisational argument —
leading a programme alongside the Institute's groups rather than inside one, with complementary
expertise from DS4DH, HUG and CASU-144 — is therefore an admissible institutional-mobility claim
under the SNSF's own definition, not a workaround.
