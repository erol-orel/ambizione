# Host: DS4DH, or stay at ISG outside Keiser's group?

## Recommendation: DS4DH

The decisive argument is not about scientific fit. It is about **what a referee can see**.

An Ambizione referee is in Basel or Berlin or Toronto. They read your CV, which says *Institute of
Global Health, University of Geneva*, and the host line, which would also say *Institute of Global
Health, University of Geneva*. They have no way to see that ISG contains several independent
groups, and no reason to investigate. **Internal group boundaries are invisible from outside;
departmental boundaries are visible.**

"Department of Radiology and Medical Informatics" needs no explanation. "Institute of Global
Health, but a different group from my previous one" needs a paragraph — and a paragraph explaining
why you are independent is a paragraph a referee reads as defensive.

Three supporting reasons:

- **Methodological fit.** DS4DH works on biomedical NLP and information retrieval, which is WP1's
  immediate neighbourhood. The evidence-extraction half of this project belongs there in a way it
  does not belong in an epidemiology institute.
- **Network coherence.** Teodoro is a GESICA partner. The host, the clinical anchor (HUG emergency)
  and the project all sit in the same collaboration, which is a cleaner story than a host in one
  community and a project in another.
- **Who the ISG alternative would be.** The realistic ISG hosts are co-authors of yours —
  Prof. Flahault on the *F1000Research* 2020 paper, Prof. Estill on several. Estill is also named
  alongside Keiser in the Horizon consortium, so hosting there would weaken both the independence
  argument *and* the delimitation in §5. Flahault is the stronger of the two, but it is still ISG
  and still a co-author.

## What would change my recommendation

**If Teodoro cannot commit to the independence terms.** He is an assistant professor; the binding
questions are whether he can offer space and computing, and whether you can **formally supervise
doctoral students** in that department, or only co-supervise. If doctoral supervision has to run
through him, much of the independence advantage evaporates and ISG under a full professor who
*can* delegate it may be better.

**If there is an ISG group with a distinct name and its own budget line** that you have not
co-published with. A formally constituted separate unit is more visible from outside than "a
different group within ISG".

## The test that settles it

Do not decide on preference. Send **both** candidates the independence paragraph from
`04-other-documents/host-institution-letters.md` and ask whether they would sign it as written:

> Dr Orel will establish and lead his own research group within the department, on a research line
> he has defined and which is distinct from the group's existing programme. He will act as
> principal investigator, recruit and supervise his own doctoral researcher(s), hold his own
> ethics approvals, and manage his own budget.

Whoever signs that without softening it is the right host. Whoever hedges has told you something
important, early and cheaply. Ask in the same message about **space, computing, and the formal
route to doctoral supervision** — those are the three things that turn the sentence into a fact.

## Worth asking: can you have both?

A primary affiliation at DS4DH with a **secondary or associated affiliation at ISG** would give
you the visible departmental move on the host line and keep the global-health community, the
seminar series and the domain colleagues. Swiss faculties often permit this. If it is available,
it is strictly better than either option alone, and it costs one email to the faculty
administration to find out.

## Consequences for the documents

The research plan §5 and §6, the statement of mobility, and the profile are all written for
DS4DH. **Switching to ISG is about thirty minutes of edits**, but three things would need
rewriting rather than find-and-replace:

- §6.1 "Disciplinary home" — the argument would become domain fit rather than methodological move.
- §5's independence paragraph — the departmental-change sentence disappears and must be replaced
  with something weaker but honest.
- The statement of mobility — its third argument currently rests on the departmental move. Without
  it, **the planned outgoing stays have to carry more weight**, which makes confirming the UNIL
  stay urgent rather than merely desirable.

That last point is worth noticing: the ISG option does not only weaken §5, it raises the stakes on
the mobility component, which is already your weakest criterion.
