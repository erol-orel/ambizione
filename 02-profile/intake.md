# Intake — what I need from you

Answer in chat or edit this file directly. **Tier 1 blocks everything**; Tier 2 and 3 can follow.
Rough answers are fine — precision comes later. Do not polish; I would rather have your
honest scribble than a tidy version of it.

---

## Tier 1 — needed before any writing

**1. Field and subfield.** In one line, plus the SNSF division you would fall under
(I: Humanities & Social Sciences · II: Mathematics, Natural & Engineering Sciences ·
III: Biology & Medicine).

> 

**2. Doctorate.** Date of thesis defence/examination, institution, thesis topic.
Any career interruptions since (parental leave, illness, service, care duties) with dates.

> 

**3. Where you are now.** Current position, institution, group/PI, since when.
Nationality, and any Swiss degrees or Swiss research periods with dates.

> 

**4. Host institution for the grant.** Which Swiss institution and department? Is it where you
are now, or a move? What is the current state of the conversation with them — nothing yet,
informal yes, or agreed?

> 

**5. The project idea.** The most important answer here. Three to ten sentences, plain language,
no grant-speak:
- What is the question?
- Why is it not already answered — what is actually blocking the field?
- What can you do about it that others cannot or have not?
- What would exist at the end of four years that does not exist now?

Write it as you would explain it to a smart colleague from a neighbouring field over coffee.
If you have several candidate ideas, give me all of them and say which excites you most —
choosing between them is part of what I can help with.

> 

**6. Independence.** How does this project differ from your PhD supervisor's and your current
PI's research programme? Be honest, including about overlaps. This is the hinge on which
Ambizione applications turn, and reviewers are good at spotting a repackaged postdoc project.

> 

---

## Tier 2 — needed for the CV, narratives and output list

**7. Publications.** Your full list, or the top 10–15 with your author position, venue, and a
one-line note on what each contributed and what your specific role was. The SNSF format caps
the output list at 10 items and asks for narrative achievements, so the selection and the
story around it matter more than the count.

> 

**8. Other achievements.** Grants and fellowships (awarded and declined), prizes, invited talks,
supervision of students, teaching, software/datasets/instruments released, patents, clinical or
policy impact, community roles (reviewing, organising, editorial), outreach.

> 

**9. Track record gaps.** Anything a reviewer might see as a weakness — a thin publication year,
a change of field, a long time to first author paper, a gap in the CV. Tell me now so we can
frame it deliberately rather than let a reviewer frame it for you.

> 

---

## Tier 3 — needed for the plan, budget and mobility statement

**10. Resources you need.** People (PhD students, postdocs — and for how long), equipment,
computing, access to cohorts/archives/facilities/field sites, consumables, travel, collaborators.
Note the total must fit **~CHF 250,000** of project funds over 4 years, on top of your salary.

> 

**11. Preliminary data / feasibility evidence.** What do you already have that shows this will
work — pilot results, a working method, an established collaboration, secured data access?

> 

**12. Mobility.** Your full mobility history (where, when, why), and any mobility planned during
the grant. If you are staying put, the scientific reason this institution is the right one.

> 

**13. Practical.** Any institutional grant-office support you already have; anyone who has
agreed to read drafts; whether you have applied to Ambizione or a similar scheme before, and if
so what feedback you received.

>
