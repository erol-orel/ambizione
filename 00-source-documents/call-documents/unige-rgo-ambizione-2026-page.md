## The binding internal facts

- **Internal deadline: fill the Formulaire RGO no later than Monday 19 October 2026** to obtain
  the vice-rector's confirmation letter. FNS deadline: Tuesday 3 November 2026.
- All support-letter requests go **through the RGO form**, not by email.
- The form requires three attachments:
  1. **The detailed support letter of the host department**, dated and signed by the **contact
     person named in the application** and by the **department director** (signed BEFORE the
     form is submitted).
  2. **CV in FNS format** (SNSF CV model).
  3. **The UNIGE ethics declaration** (declaration-ethique.unige.ch).
- Once received, the RGO submits the confirmation to the vice-rector for signature and returns
  it to the applicant.

## Procedure in order

1. Download the host-department confirmation letter (the SNSF template; already in this folder).
2. Have it filled and signed by the contact person and the department/institute director.
3. Fill the Formulaire RGO with the three attachments, by 19 October.
