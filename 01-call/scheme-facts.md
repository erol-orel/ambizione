## Purpose and shape of the grant

`[HIGH]` Ambizione funds early-career researchers who want to run their **own independent
project, with their own team, at a Swiss higher education institution**. The scheme is
explicitly a step towards scientific independence — this framing matters, because a proposal
that reads like a continuation of the postdoc supervisor's programme is the single most common
way these applications fail.

`[HIGH]` Duration: **maximum 4 years, minimum 2 years**.

`[HIGH]` Funding for the 2026 call: the grantee's **salary** (including social security
contributions) plus **project funds capped at CHF 250,000** for a four-year grant — down from the
earlier CHF 400,000 ceiling. Budget against the 2026 figure, not older examples.

`[HIGH]` **Doctoral students and postdocs cannot be employed from Ambizione project funds.** This
changed from the 2025 call onwards; employment of *other* staff (technical and scientific
assistants) remains possible. Confirmed independently against the official guidelines
(version 11 August 2026) and against university grant-office summaries. The practical consequence
is large: **the project must be PI-led**, and any proposal budgeting a doctoral researcher is
building on a rule that no longer exists.

`[CONFIRMED]` (Guidelines 2.9) Average gross salary **around CHF 115,000/year at 100%**, employer social contributions covered; exact figure set with UNIGE HR (older
guidance said ~CHF 105,000). Check the current figure in the call document.

`[CONFIRMED]` (Art. 2) Two variants exist: an **Ambizione grant with salary**, and an
**Ambizione project grant** where the host institution pays the salary and the SNSF covers only
project costs. Confirm both are still offered in 2026.

## Deadline

`[HIGH]` **3 November 2026** — submission via mySNF. Call opened 3 August 2026.

`[HIGH]` This is the **final** Ambizione call. The scheme is being phased out in favour of a
new postdoctoral funding instrument launching in 2028. There is no "apply again next year".

## Eligibility

`[HIGH]` Doctorate (PhD) completed, **or** completed medical studies (Staatsexamen or equivalent).

`[HIGH]` **Not more than 4 years** since the doctorate at the submission deadline. The relevant
date is the **examination or defence of the doctoral thesis**, not the graduation ceremony or
the diploma date.

`[HIGH]` Medical route: applicants who completed the medical exam and did **at least 3 years of
clinical work** afterwards may apply up to **9 years** after the medical exam.

`[HIGH]` **At least 12 months of research activity** after the doctorate (or after the medical
licence) is required.

`[HIGH]` **Connection to Switzerland** — satisfy at least one of:
- Swiss nationality; or
- a Swiss degree (e.g. master's or doctorate); or
- at least 12 months of research at a Swiss research institution between completing the
  doctorate/medical diploma and the submission deadline.

`[HIGH]` **Professors are not eligible** — permanent or temporary, with or without tenure track.

`[CONFIRMED]` (Art. 5 §6 → GIR Clause 1.11) Extensions to the 4-year window exist for care duties, illness, military service,
and similar interruptions; the SNSF has adapted these criteria recently. If any interruption
applies to you, claim it — it is not automatic and it is routinely under-claimed.

## Required application documents

`[HIGH]` Submitted through **mySNF**. Some content is entered directly in the web form; the
research plan, CV and research output list are uploaded as PDFs.

| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Research plan | `[CONFIRMED]` (Guidelines 4.3) max **15 pages AND 60,000 characters with spaces** (title, summary, tables, figures, footnotes included; bibliography excluded). Single PDF, no annexes, min. 10 pt, 1.5 spacing, **no website links**, prescribed section structure, **mySNF counter binding**. |
| CV | `[HIGH]` Must use the **SNSF CV format** (narrative-based, not a classic academic CV). |
| Research output list | `[VERIFY]` Max **10 works in total** across all achievements, distributed as you choose. |
| Achievement narratives | `[VERIFY]` Max **4,350 characters** (~1 A4 page) per narrative. |
| Statement of mobility | `[HIGH]` **New/emphasised requirement.** Justify the choice of research institution and present past and planned mobility. **This is evaluated.** |
| Detailed confirmation letter (host institution) | `[HIGH]` From the host institute/department. |
| General confirmation letter (host institution) | `[HIGH]` From the institution's central level (e.g. VP for Research). |
| Data management plan | `[HIGH]` Entered **directly in the mySNF form**, not uploaded. Treated as a **draft and excluded from evaluation** at submission; definitive DMP due by project end. |
| Budget | `[HIGH]` Entered in mySNF. |

`[HIGH]` **A cover letter and a career plan are NO LONGER required.** If you find a template or
a colleague's example that includes them, it is out of date — files uploaded for these are
deleted.

## Evaluation

`[HIGH]` Assessed by external referees and co-referees, then compared and discussed by an
**evaluation panel**.

`[HIGH]` Scientific criteria applied to the project: **scientific relevance, topicality,
originality, feasibility, and suitability of the methods**.

`[HIGH]` The research plan is *also* used to assess the **applicant's own scientific
qualifications**, particularly their expertise in relation to the project. The plan is a
document about you as much as about the science.

`[HIGH]` **Mobility is an assessment criterion** — hence the mandatory statement of mobility.

`[HIGH]` Where a group of applications is of **equal quality and equally worthy of funding**
and no further differentiation is possible, the decision within that group is made by
**drawing lots** (Art. 19 §6 of the Organisational Regulations of the Research Council).
Practical consequence: the goal is not to be "fundable" but to be clearly *above* the tie-break
band. A proposal that lands in the lottery zone has already lost most of its agency.

`[CONFIRMED]` (Guidelines 2.18) Two-phase evaluation with an **interview** for phase-2 applicants: presentation + panel Q&A. **Life Sciences panels: 3–4 June 2027.** Phase-1 outcome early April 2027; final decision early August 2027. Equal-quality tie-breaks may be decided **by drawing lots**.
Ambizione has historically been decided on the written dossier, unlike Eccellenza/PRIMA.
Confirm this — it changes how much preparation the autumn/winter needs.
