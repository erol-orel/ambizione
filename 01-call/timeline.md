# Timeline — working backwards from 3 November 2026

As of **18 August 2026** there are roughly **11 weeks** to the deadline. That is enough for a
strong application and not enough for a leisurely one. The binding constraints are not your
writing speed — they are other people's calendars: host-institution signatures, and the
colleagues whose critical reading is what actually raises the score.

| When | What | Why it is on this date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Week of 18 Aug** | Eligibility confirmed. Host institution and host professor/department agreed in principle. Core idea locked to one paragraph. | Everything downstream depends on these. An unresolved host is a project-killer, not a detail. |
| **Week of 25 Aug** | Formally request the **detailed confirmation letter** from the host institute. Contact the central research office about the **general confirmation letter** and ask for their internal deadline in writing. | Institutional lead times are weeks. ETH, for example, has been asking for the detailed letter plus CV by **21 October**; other institutions differ. Ask, do not assume. |
| **Week of 25 Aug – 8 Sep** | Research plan: state of the art + own prior work + objectives. First full draft of work packages. | The intellectual core. Do it while you are freshest. |
| **8 – 22 Sep** | Complete research plan draft (all sections, bibliography, figures). Budget drafted against the ~CHF 250k ceiling. | You want a complete bad draft by late September, not a perfect half draft. |
| **~22 Sep** | **Send the full draft to 2–3 readers**: one in-field senior colleague, one out-of-field researcher, ideally one former SNSF panel member. Give them 2 weeks and specific questions. | This is the single highest-return step in the process, and it only works if you leave time to act on what comes back. |
| **22 Sep – 6 Oct** | While readers have it: SNSF-format CV and achievement narratives, research output list (max 10), statement of mobility, DMP content, budget detail. | Parallelise. These do not depend on the research-plan feedback. |
| **6 – 20 Oct** | Revise the research plan on the feedback. This is a rewrite, not a polish. | Reviewers' first reading is the only one that counts. |
| **~20 Oct** | Host-institution letters **in hand**. All mySNF form fields entered. | Buffer against institutional slippage. |
| **20 – 30 Oct** | Final read-through against `05-review/self-assessment.md`. Formatting compliance check (pages, characters, font, spacing, single PDF, no annexes). | Formal non-compliance is a rejection route that costs nothing to avoid. |
| **~30 Oct** | **Submit.** | Do not submit on 3 November. mySNF load and last-minute institutional signature problems are real. Three days of buffer is the cheapest insurance in the process. |

## Standing risks to watch

- **Host-institution letters** — the most common cause of a missed deadline. Chase weekly.
- **The CHF 250k ceiling** — if your project only works at CHF 400k, you have a design problem
  to solve now, not a budget to trim in October.
- **Final call.** There is no next round. Plan the schedule with no reliance on a second attempt.
