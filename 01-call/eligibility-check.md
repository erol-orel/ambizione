Work through this and record the answer. Every year, applicants spend three months on a
research plan and are then declared ineligible on a date arithmetic error.

## Extensions to the 4-year window — claim these if they apply

These are not applied automatically. If any of the following interrupted your research, they
can extend the window; document them in mySNF.

- [ ] Maternity / paternity / parental leave
- [ ] Care duties (children, dependent relatives)
- [ ] Illness or accident
- [ ] Military or civil service
- [ ] Other documented non-research interruption: `________`

`[VERIFY]` Confirm the exact extension rates (e.g. months granted per child) in the current
Ambizione regulations — the SNSF has revised these recently.

## The mobility question — decide early, it shapes the whole application

Mobility is an **assessment criterion** and the statement of mobility is evaluated. The
structural question:

> **Are you applying to stay where you currently are, or to move?**

Staying at your current institution is permitted but it is the harder case: you must make a
positive, specific argument that this institution is where *this project* has to happen, not
merely where you happen to be. If the honest answer is "because my life is here", you still
need a scientific argument alongside it — and you should consider whether a move, or a
credibly documented plan for outgoing/incoming mobility during the grant, strengthens the case.
