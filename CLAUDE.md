# Project rules

- **Never use an em-dash (the U+2014 character) in any file in this repository, in any language.** This is a
  permanent instruction from the applicant. In the research plan use real punctuation instead
  (colon, semicolon, comma, or parentheses); in notes and emails a spaced hyphen ( - ) is fine.
  En-dashes in numeric ranges (2015–2026, M3–M9) are allowed.
- The research plan's scientific text is frozen (30 Sep 2026). Science changes only if two
  external readers independently converge on the same problem. See `TODO.md`.
- `03-research-plan/FINAL-research-plan.md` is generated by `draft/assemble.sh`; never edit it
  by hand. TWO limits bind, per Guidelines 4.3: 60,000 characters (mySNF counter binding,
  check `draft/wordcount.sh`) AND **15 A4 pages at minimum 10pt with 1.5 line spacing**,
  bibliography excluded from both (check `draft/pagecheck.sh`). The page cap is the tighter
  one; the plan is FROZEN at 50,276 characters / exactly 15 pages (7 Oct, commit 06b7e11, after 18
  external audit rounds; restyled in plain language on 13 Oct at the applicant's request, 51,320
  characters / exactly 15 pages, science unchanged; margins at the declared floor, ~1.0 cm top/bottom, 1.2 cm sides). Any addition must re-verify pages. `draft/make-submission.sh` builds the formatted docx/PDF.
- Never invent citations or author lists; flag what cannot be verified.
- No model identifiers in committed artifacts. Commit messages end with the attribution footer
  given in the session instructions.
- **Applicant's style preference (13 Oct): simple, plain terms.** When proposing rewordings for
  the own-words pass, prefer everyday words that are easy to grasp over technical shorthand
  (e.g. "checked against what then happens" rather than "prospectively verified against observed
  outcomes"), while keeping the exact meaning. Keep a technical term only where it is the
  field's precise word and no plain equivalent exists; then explain it once.
- **Applicant's style preference (13 Oct): avoid colons and semicolons inside sentences.** Prefer
  commas, "and", or two short sentences. This applies to rewordings proposed for the own-words
  pass and to new text; existing colons/semicolons in the plan are flagged in the review pass of
  the applicant's version, not rewritten silently. (Overrides the older advice above to use a
  colon or semicolon in place of an em-dash: use a comma, parentheses or a new sentence instead.)
